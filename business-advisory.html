In detail

What the engagement covers.

What the work involves, and the business situations that typically bring it about.

What it involves

  • Financial decision-making support
  • Cost management and margin review
  • Business planning and expansion-decision analysis
  • Performance analysis against internal or industry benchmarks
  • Process improvement recommendations
  • Make-versus-buy and outsourcing evaluation
  • Independent advisory grounded in financial modelling, not opinion

Business situations

  • A significant business decision under consideration
  • A business seeking an independent view before committing
  • A promoter weighing options without an in-house finance function
  • A business reviewing performance against its own expectations
  • A business evaluating a new revenue stream, product line or market

Value. Advisory grounded in the actual numbers gives management a clearer basis for a decision — the analysis, not a recommendation dressed up as certainty.
