Banking & Finance Support

Support in preparing structured financial and business information for discussions with lenders and financial institutions — not a promise of the outcome of those discussions.

Request a consultation All capabilities

What this covers

Scope of the engagement.

What the work involves, and the business situations that typically bring it about.

What it involves

  • Financial information preparation
  • Business and project documentation
  • Financial projections and supporting schedules
  • Working-capital analysis
  • Funding requirement assessment
  • Executive summaries, organised for lender review
  • Term loan and working-capital facility documentation
  • Collateral and security documentation coordination

Business situations

  • A business preparing to approach a bank or financial institution
  • A business arranging or restructuring debt facilities
  • A business whose existing documentation is scattered or incomplete
  • A promoter preparing for a funding conversation for the first time
  • A business refinancing existing debt on better terms
  • A business consolidating facilities across multiple lenders

Value

What this is intended to deliver.

Organised, complete documentation is what allows a lender's own process to move — our role is to help the business arrive at that conversation prepared.
