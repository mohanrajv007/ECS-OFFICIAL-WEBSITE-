In detail

What the engagement covers.

What the work involves, and the business situations that typically bring it about.

What it involves

  • Financial due diligence and accounting review
  • Quality of earnings analysis
  • Revenue and cost analysis
  • Working capital analysis and normalisation
  • Cash-flow review
  • Identification of financial risks and exceptions
  • Net debt and completion-accounts considerations
  • Due diligence reporting for transaction decision-making

Business situations

  • A prospective buyer evaluating a target business before a transaction
  • A business undergoing due diligence ahead of a sale or investment
  • A lender or investor requiring independent financial review before committing funds
  • A transaction where reported earnings need independent verification
  • A business needing working capital and cash flow scrutinised before terms are finalised

Value. Independent due diligence gives both sides of a transaction a common, evidence-based understanding of the numbers — reducing the risk of surprises after the deal is done.
